Indigenous People's Sunrise Gathering

We boarded the 5 a.m. boat to Alcatraz on October 13th for the Indigenous People’s Day sunrise gathering, continuing a tradition that began with the 1969–1971 Occupation of the Island. The occupation helped spark the modern Indigenous rights movement.
A bonfire burned in Alcatraz.
Representatives from many Nations shared prayers, songs, and dances beneath the rising sun.
"We are here to honor truth in history".
Before the bars, there was land.
Indigenous people have been here from times immemorial.
They’re still here.
